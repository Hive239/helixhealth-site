Subscriptions & billing

Helix Fitness offers a free tier plus two subscriptions: Basic (readiness, wearables, and full tracking) and Premium (adds the AI coach and AI meal scan). Purchases, renewals, and refunds are handled by Apple.

  • Manage or cancel: iPhone → Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Helix Fitness.
  • Free trials: cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends to avoid being charged.
  • Refunds: request through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Restore a purchase: open the paywall in the app and tap “Restore Purchases.”

Connecting your data

Helix Fitness syncs with Apple Health (your Apple Watch and anything that writes to Apple Health). If a metric isn’t showing up, open iPhone → Settings → Apps → Health → Data Access & Devices → Helix and make sure the permissions are on, then reopen the app.

Your data & privacy

Your data stays on your device. Helix has no account and no server that stores your health information. You can export or delete everything in Settings → Data. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Not medical advice

Helix Fitness provides general fitness and wellness information generated in part by AI. It is not medical advice and does not replace professional care. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider before changing your exercise, nutrition, or supplement routine.
