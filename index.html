What it does

One system for training, recovery, and fuel.

Helix pulls every signal your body already produces into one place, then makes the call, so you stop guessing whether to push or back off.

Readiness engine

Blends your Apple Health data (Apple Watch and anything that syncs to it) into a single recovery score with a next-day forecast, built on your own baselines, not a generic average. It flags overtraining, sleep debt, and when your signals disagree.

AI coach

A strength & recovery coach that sees your full picture (sleep, strain, nutrition, PRs) and answers in plain language.

Adaptive workouts

Your program eases reps to today's readiness (never your sets or weight), auto-tracks PRs, and logs to Apple Health.

Nutrition, measured

Snap a photo or describe a meal for instant calories, macros, and micros, grounded in USDA data, plus a 307-supplement reference library.

Recovery & mind

Track sleep debt and HRV trends, get calorie burn from real heart-rate data, and run EEG-guided meditation with a Muse headband. The calm side of getting stronger.

How it works

Connect once. Decide daily.

Connect your signals

Link Apple Health and any wearables you already own. Helix learns your personal baselines over the first few days.

Get your readiness

Every morning, Helix computes one recovery score and a forecast, and explains what's driving it, with no cherry-picked metrics.

Train, fuel, recover

Follow the session it tunes to your readiness, log meals in seconds, and let the coach answer whatever you ask.
